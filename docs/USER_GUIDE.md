# 661C Home Hub: how to use it

## The idea in one minute

* A **required item** is something the home needs: "Main sofa", "Dining pendant", "Kitchen sink".
  All 193 rows from your workbook are already here, room by room.
* An **option** is a product you found for that item: a screenshot, photo or link. An item can
  have as many options as you like.
* Exactly **one option per item can be approved**. Only approved options count as committed money.
  Shortlisted options never do.
* **Payments** are separate. Approving isn't paying, and paying a deposit doesn't add to what's
  committed. It reduces what's still owed.

## Saving something you found (the + button)

1. Tap **+** (bottom right).
2. **Screenshots** (pick one or several) or **Take photo**, and/or paste a link.
3. Tap the **room**, then the **item**, or **New item** if it isn't on the list.
4. Tap **Save**. That's it. Name, price and details are optional and can be added later.

Several screenshots at once? Choose **"3 different products"** (one option each) or **"One
product, 3 photos"**.

No signal in a showroom? Save anyway. It waits on your phone and uploads itself later.

## Prices: be honest about what you know

Each price says what kind it is: **Seen online (not a quote)**, **Estimate**, **Supplier
quotation** or **Confirmed final price**. If you're not sure of the currency (for example a "$"
on a Chinese site), leave the currency as **Not sure**. The app won't guess, won't convert it,
and won't let it be approved until the currency is confirmed.

Foreign prices use the exchange rate under **Budget → Exchange rates** (fetch a reference rate or
type the rate your bank or Alipay actually gave you). Approved costs and payments keep the rate
they were saved with.

## Deciding together

* Open an item, then **Compare**: photos side by side with price, landed cost, size, material,
  warranty, open checks, and each person's vote.
* Both of you can vote (**Love it / Like / Unsure / Not for us**) and comment on any option.
* The owner taps **Approve**. Approving a different option later **replaces** the previous approval;
  the old one stays in the history but no longer counts.

## Before ordering

Each option gets a checklist for its type (sofa: dimensions, chaise side, fit behind the coffee-bar
island, lift and door access; lighting: 230 V, neutral-white 4000K, ceiling support; toilets: S/P-trap,
rough-in, PUB/WELS; and so on). Mark each check **Confirmed / Not applicable / Failed** and note the
evidence. "Mark as ordered" is held back while critical checks are open, unless you write down why
you're going ahead anyway.

These checks are reminders, not certification. The app never claims a product meets Singapore rules.

## Warranties

Record what the seller says and tick **"I have seen the written terms"** only when you have.
A China warranty doesn't mean repairs are available in Singapore. The *Valid in Singapore*,
*Local servicing* and *Parts* fields exist for exactly this.

## Paying

From the approved choice, tap **Record payment**: deposit, progress, balance, full, or refund. If
you switch to another option after paying a deposit, that deposit stays in **Actual paid** and
appears as "Paid towards replaced choices". Record a refund if you get it back.

## The budget page in plain words

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total budget | What you've set aside. |
| Forecast final cost | Approved choices, plus your allowance (or the highest shortlisted price) for everything else, plus shared costs and contingency. |
| Approved commitments | The one approved option per item. |
| Actual paid | Money actually paid, minus refunds. |
| Outstanding | Approved cost minus what's been paid on it. |
| Remaining vs commitments | Budget minus what's approved or already spent. |
| Remaining vs forecast | Budget minus the forecast. If negative, the plan is over budget. |

Tap any figure to see the items and payments behind it.

**Container freight:** add it once under **Budget → Shared costs**. It's split across approved
China items for the room view, but counted only once. Leave the per-option freight blank for
container items so freight isn't counted twice.

## The Guangzhou / Foshan trip

**Shop → Guangzhou & Foshan guide**: each destination lists the items to look for, photos you've
saved, suppliers already contacted, and what still needs a quotation. Map buttons open Amap,
Baidu or Apple Maps by Chinese name, and *Copy Chinese name* is handy for DiDi or taxis.
Addresses are only stored once you've verified them. See `CHINA_ACCESS.md` for connectivity tips.

## Keith

Keith can see everything, add screenshots and options, shortlist, vote and comment. He can't
approve, record payments or change budgets, unless the owner switches those on under
**More → People & permissions**.

## Your workbook

After the import, the app is the main record. Re-importing the workbook later is safe: rows are
matched by ID, nothing is duplicated, and anything you changed in the app is kept (and listed).
**More → Export to Excel** gives you a full backup at any time.
